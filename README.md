# Jal-Jeevan-2.0
Offline-First Water Supply Fault Detection and Fair Distribution Monitor
# Problem Statement

Many rural areas face difficulties in monitoring water supply faults, leakage, and unequal water distribution.

# Proposed Solution

Jal-Jeevan 2.0 is an offline-first water supply monitoring concept designed to identify possible supply faults and support fair water distribution.

# Key Features

Water supply fault monitoring

Leakage detection concept

Fair water distribution monitoring

Offline-first operation

Support for rural water management

# Objective

To improve water supply monitoring, reduce water wastage, and support reliable access to water in rural communities.

# Project Status

Concept and documentation uploaded. Prototype development and testing are pending.

# Target Users

Rural communities, village water management teams, and local authorities.
